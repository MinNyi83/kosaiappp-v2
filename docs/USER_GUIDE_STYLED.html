1. System Overview

KosAI is a cloud-based field service management platform designed for CCTV, networking, WiFi, and NAS infrastructure companies. It connects office administrators, field technicians, and customers through a unified system.

User Roles

RoleAccessLogin Method
AdminFull system access — all modulesUsername + Password or Google Sign-In
TechnicianMobile app — jobs, checklist, history, settingsEmployee ID (TECH-xxx) + PIN
SalesLimited admin — clients and jobsEmployee ID + PIN
ClientPortal — service history, warranty, invoicesClient ID lookup

Access Points

PlatformURLPurpose
Admin Consoleadmin.htmlOffice management dashboard
Technician Appapp.htmlField operations (mobile-first)
Client Portalportal.htmlCustomer self-service
Landing Pageindex.htmlPublic marketing site
Desktop App.exe (Tauri)Offline-capable Windows application

2. Getting Started

First-Time Admin Login

1
Open admin.html in your browser (or launch the desktop app)
2
Click "Sign In" on the lock screen
3
Enter your Username and Password
4
Click "Sign In"
Default Admin Account Username: admin | Password: (set during initial setup). If you forgot your password, use System Settings > Database Restore from a backup.

First-Time Technician Login

1
Open app.html on your phone (or use the PWA install prompt)
2
Enter your Employee ID (e.g., TECH-001) and PIN (default: 1234)
3
Tap "Sign In"

Changing Your PIN (Technician)

1
Go to Settings tab in the mobile app
2
Tap "Change PIN"
3
Enter your current PIN, then your new PIN twice
4
Tap "Update PIN"

5. Dispatch Map

Admin Module: Dashboard > Dispatch Map

An interactive Leaflet.js map showing real-time technician locations.

Features

  • Technician Markers — Each technician appears as a colored marker on the map
  • Click a Marker — Shows technician name, current status, and assigned job
  • GPS Tracking — Technicians share location when they clock in
  • HQ Marker — Your head office location is always shown
  • Dark Mode — Map tiles automatically adjust to dark/light theme

HQ Configuration

1
Go to System Settings
2
Scroll to "Head Office Location"
3
Enter your office name, Google Maps URL, coordinates, and address
4
Click "Save"

16. AI Copilot

Admin Module: Dashboard > AI Copilot

Three Tools

A. Chat with Data

Ask natural language questions about your business data. Examples:

  • "How many jobs were completed this month?"
  • "Which technician has the highest completion rate?"
  • "What is our total revenue this quarter?"

B. Auto-Dispatcher

AI matches technicians to jobs based on skills, workload, proximity, and past performance. Select a job > click "Auto-Assign" > review suggestion > "Confirm".

C. Route Optimizer

Select multiple jobs > click "Optimize Route" > view optimal visit sequence on the map.

26. Troubleshooting

IssueSolution
Cannot log in (Admin)Verify credentials, check role is "Admin", try Google Sign-In
Cannot log in (Technician)Use format TECH-xxx, default PIN 1234, check account is Active
Photos not uploadingCheck internet, verify Google Drive integration, check Telegram bot token
Jobs not appearingPull to refresh, verify correct account, check job assignment
Attendance not recordingEnable GPS/location, check internet, try Telegram as backup
AI Copilot not respondingCheck GEMINI_API_KEY, verify quota, dual-gateway fallback active
Telegram bot silentVerify bot token and chat ID, test with /start
Offline mode (Technician)Jobs/checklists cached locally, photos queued, tap Sync when online
